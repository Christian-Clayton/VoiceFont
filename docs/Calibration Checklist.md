# VoiceFont Calibration Checklist

**Document type:** reference checklist for the person recording their own authorised voice.

Version 1.0.0 | Language en-GB | 75 prompts | 54 core, 21 optional

Use your own natural accent, not an imitation. These original prompts sample everyday sounds and delivery choices; they cannot capture every facet of a voice and do not test identity, emotion or pronunciation. Stay within a comfortable speaking range, take breaks and skip any task that feels uncomfortable. Record only your own voice or one you have explicit permission to use.

## Before recording

- [ ] Confirm permission to record and use this voice locally.
- [ ] Choose a quiet room and keep the microphone at a steady distance.
- [ ] Speak in your natural accent. Pause, drink water or stop whenever needed.
- [ ] Read only the prompt text, not its delivery instruction.
- [ ] Replay a short take before continuing. Reduce input gain if clipped; move closer if too quiet.
- [ ] Save each take before changing prompts. Saved takes survive reload; unsaved browser audio does not.
- [ ] Use Export backup to keep a ZIP of the session and raw takes.

Coverage means recorded prompts, not verified phoneme accuracy or captured vocal ability. Partial finalisation needs at least three accepted prompts from two categories. Skipped and optional prompts remain visible; no exhaustive coverage is promised.

## Questions and emphasis (8)

Use meaning to shape the sentence. Capitals indicate a suggested focus, not extra volume.

- [ ] **questions-01** (core, neutral)
  - Say: Did you leave the window open?
  - Delivery: Ask a genuine yes-or-no question, not an accusation.
  - Intended dimensions: question:yes-no

- [ ] **questions-02** (core, neutral)
  - Say: Where did you put the spare key?
  - Delivery: Ask as if you genuinely need the information.
  - Intended dimensions: question:wh

- [ ] **questions-03** (core, neutral)
  - Say: Would you prefer tea, coffee, or a glass of water?
  - Delivery: Offer three choices with natural list intonation.
  - Intended dimensions: question:choice, list

- [ ] **questions-04** (core, curious)

  - Delivery: Sound mildly curious. Use the question tune natural to you.
  - Intended dimensions: question:tag

- [ ] **questions-05** (core, neutral)

  - Delivery: Emphasise I: someone else understood differently.
  - Intended dimensions: stress:subject

- [ ] **questions-06** (core, neutral)
  - Say: I thought you meant the small box.
  - Delivery: Emphasise small: the size was the misunderstanding.
  - Intended dimensions: stress:adjective

- [ ] **questions-07** (core, neutral)

  - Intended dimensions: stress:time

- [ ] **questions-08** (core, warm)

## Everyday conversation (10)

Speak as if talking with someone you know. Use the supplied text so repeated recordings remain comparable.

- [ ] **conversation-01** (core, warm)
  - Say: Hello, it is good to hear from you. How has your week been?
  - Delivery: Speak as if starting a relaxed phone call.
  - Intended dimensions: greeting, turn-taking

- [ ] **conversation-02** (core, casual)
  - Say: Yes, that works for me. Shall I bring something for lunch?
  - Delivery: Imagine replying to a friendly invitation.
  - Intended dimensions: response, follow-up

- [ ] **conversation-03** (core, casual)
  - Say: Sorry, I missed the last part. Could you say the time again?
  - Delivery: Make a polite request for clarification.
  - Intended dimensions: repair, question

- [ ] **conversation-04** (core, neutral)
  - Say: Let me think for a moment. I believe we turned left after the bridge.
  - Delivery: Allow a short, natural thinking pause between sentences.
  - Intended dimensions: hesitation, recollection

- [ ] **conversation-05** (core, warm)

  - Delivery: Speak as if returning with a cup of tea.
  - Intended dimensions: thanks, explanation

- [ ] **conversation-06** (core, casual)

  - Delivery: Disagree gently at a normal volume.
  - Intended dimensions: disagreement, contrast

- [ ] **conversation-07** (core, neutral)

  - Intended dimensions: instruction, sequence

- [ ] **conversation-08** (core, neutral)
